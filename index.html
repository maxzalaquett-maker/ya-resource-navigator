About this directory

An expanded operating baseline—not a promise of availability

What is included

Government agencies, public institutions, established health systems and established nonprofit providers with a defined service or intake process.

What is excluded

Unverified informal aid, crowd-sourced listings without a primary source and organizations without a clear way to request service.

Update rule

Recheck housing waitlists, shelter beds, emergency funding, workforce cohorts, treatment capacity and pilot programs before every referral.

30-mile search area

“Local / nearby” means the organization is located in or clearly serves a community within roughly 30 road miles of Uptown Charlotte. It is a practical service-area screen, not a precise mileage guarantee. Always confirm county, ZIP-code and residency rules.

Mentorship and soft skills

Use the Support focus filter to find mentoring, leadership, communication, workplace readiness, financial capability and independent-living development. Some youth programs require enrollment before age 18, which is stated in the resource caveat.
