About this directory

About the Charlotte young adult resource directory

This directory is a practical starting point for young adults and the people helping them find trusted local support.

Important directory disclaimer

This directory is provided for general informational and referral purposes only. Inclusion does not constitute an endorsement or guarantee of a provider, and it does not mean a provider will accept a referral, have openings, offer a particular service, or determine someone eligible. Programs, eligibility, funding, service areas, contact information and availability can change without notice. The directory does not control third-party providers or their decisions. Contact each provider directly to confirm current information and decide whether a resource is appropriate for your situation.

Who this directory is for

Young adults can use the directory themselves. It can also help social workers, case managers, mentors, foster families, churches, nonprofits and other community members find possible referrals for a young adult who needs support.

What is included

Government agencies, public institutions, established health systems and established nonprofit providers with a defined service or intake process.

What is excluded

Unverified informal aid, crowd-sourced listings without a primary source and organizations without a clear way to request service.

Information changes

Information is reviewed periodically, but listings can become outdated between reviews. Recheck eligibility, housing waitlists, shelter beds, emergency funding, workforce cohorts, treatment capacity, contact information and current availability directly with the provider before relying on a listing.

Charlotte-area coverage

“Local / nearby” means the organization is located in or clearly serves Charlotte, Mecklenburg County or a community within roughly 30 road miles of Uptown Charlotte. It is a practical service-area screen, not a precise mileage guarantee. Always confirm county, ZIP-code and residency rules.

Mentorship and life skills

Use the Support area filter to find mentoring, leadership, communication, workplace readiness, financial capability and independent-living development. Some youth programs require enrollment before age 18, which is stated in the resource caveat.
